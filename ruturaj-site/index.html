TEMPLE RECESSION — HAIR PROTOCOL
Evidence-based. Zero testosterone disruption. Safe long-term approach.
Temple recession = androgenetic alopecia (genetic DHT sensitivity). The hair follicles are miniaturizing due to DHT binding. Treatment works by (1) increasing blood flow to follicles, (2) blocking DHT at the follicle level, and (3) stimulating new growth. The good news: temples respond well to topical treatment when caught early. ✅ None of these recommendations affect systemic testosterone levels.
💊
MINOXIDIL 5% TOPICAL — CORE TREATMENT
FDA APPROVED
How to Apply
Apply 1ml (about 20–25 drops OR half dropper) directly on temples + slightly above. Massage with fingertips for 1 minute. Let dry completely (30 min) before going to bed.
Timing
Evening only (before bed). Do NOT apply morning — it affects styling, and daytime sweat washes it off before absorption.
Timeline
Weeks 1–8: Initial shedding (normal! old miniaturized hairs falling out). Months 3–4: New growth visible. Month 6: Significant density improvement at temples.
Testosterone?
✅ Safe — Topical minoxidil has zero systemic hormonal effects. It works by opening potassium channels in follicle cells to increase blood flow. No testosterone, no DHT, no hormones involved.
Recommended brands (India): Mintop 5% (Dr Reddy's) ₹250–300 / Tugain 5% (Cipla) ₹280–350 / Rogaine 5% (J&J) ₹600–800. All same active ingredient. Mintop or Tugain are cheapest and most proven.

Almonds, walnuts, sunflower seeds, oats, bananas. Or supplement: 2500–5000 mcg/day biotin. Safe, water-soluble.

Low iron = hair fall. Spinach, lentils, chana, rajma, tofu. Eat with Vitamin C source to increase absorption.

Get blood tested: Check Ferritin, Vitamin D3, Vitamin B12, Zinc, TSH (thyroid). Deficiencies in any of these directly cause hair loss. This is the most common reason young men in India lose hair. If levels are low, supplementation alone can reverse thinning.
🚫 WHAT DOES NOT WORK (Save your money)
Finasteride / Dutasteride (oral) — Blocks 5-alpha reductase → reduces systemic DHT. YES it works for hair. But YES it can affect testosterone, libido, and mood (Post-Finasteride Syndrome in ~2% users). Avoid unless prescribed by dermatologist after bloodwork.
Onion juice / garlic / ginger — Anecdotal only. No proper RCT evidence. Messy, smelly, no proven results. Skip.
Hair oils (alone, without massage) — Oil by itself doesn't penetrate follicle. The massage is what works. Oil just helps you massage without friction.
Expensive shampoos claiming "hair regrowth" — Shampoo is on your scalp for 2 minutes and rinses off. Cannot regrow hair. Use a gentle sulfate-free shampoo (Dove/Dove Men / Sebamed) and spend money on treatment instead.
